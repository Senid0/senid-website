Reflection One: AI Can Help, but It Should Not Do the Thinking

From this article alone, I can see that the LLM group is impacted negatively compared to the search engine group and the brain-only group. For instance, the LLM group was affected because participants were not thinking about what they were doing, causing them to fall “behind in their ability to quote from the essays they wrote just minutes prior.” This shows that AI is not really teaching us anything; it is basically taking our brain power away, which can possibly affect the way we live our lives.

To be honest, I feel like AI is only okay if you don't know where to start, as a way to brainstorm a topic or something. It should not be used to complete anything because it is not your own thinking. When AI does something for you, people often don't even know what was written by the AI. I think AI should only be used to help your thinking and not to do the thinking for you. As said in the article, “the LLM group's participants performed worse than their counterparts in the Brain-only group at all levels: neural, linguistic, scoring.”

We can see a major difference between the LLM group and the Brain-only group because the results show the LLM group's performance declining. For example, if I asked AI to write an essay about the history of America and just turned it in, I would not really care about the feedback because I did not write it. Writing the paper myself would help me believe I can do anything I put my mind to. It is better to be honest than to let AI do your work for you.

AI can sometimes be used to brainstorm or get help, but I believe it should not be used to do all the thinking. AI can be vague or inaccurate, and people may copy its ideas without really doing the task themselves.

Reflection Two: Photography as Memory, Meaning, and Expression

If I capture a photo or create graphics, it is usually because those photos hold on to many memories. If someone did not capture photos, there would be nothing to look back on. It means a lot to me and my family to take as many photos or videos as we can. I realized that although taking a photo can seem simple, camera position and lighting are important. I notice this when I take a photo of my parents because the photo can look really good or not so good depending on the camera position.

When I use video images to create videos for social media, I tend to edit in text, video effects, and sometimes overlays. If it were just a basic video, many people might click away, so quality matters. Although quality matters, the most important thing to me is how I feel when I am doing something I love. It means a lot to me to capture images and videos and create graphics.

I believe it is important to be happy when capturing or editing images in order to show your passion. If you do not show passion in your photos, they can seem less expressive and personal. Showing passion makes what you capture more meaningful. Photos can also show how technology and social media are part of everyday life. For instance, a photo of people using phones or laptops at Starbucks could show how technology connects people, but also how people may be less connected with each other in public spaces.

Images can show how the world has changed and can help share issues across the world. I have seen photos of pollution in the ocean that show how much trash people dump there and how it can impact Earth. One problem is that many people ignore issues expressed through photography. Even so, I believe photos can be a powerful way to communicate ideas and show important problems.

Reflection Three: Heela’s Story: Consent, Power, and the Cost of Harm

I chose “Heela: The Schoolgirl: My Stolen Future,” and it was interesting how a man could impregnate a schoolgirl and then leave when he learned she was pregnant. Heela had to think about whether to keep the baby after Patrick raped her. At first, she did not tell anyone, but she eventually told her friend that she had not had her period in three months. Her friend thought she might be pregnant, and Heela began to panic. Her grandmother noticed she was acting differently, and Heela eventually told her. They contacted the police, who helped her get counseling.

It is upsetting to think that Patrick forced her into a situation she did not plan for, and now she has to deal with what happens next. Patrick invited her into his home. She declined at first, but she was pressured into entering. He kept her from leaving until they had sex. I sometimes wonder why there are men who act like this, because even as a man, I would never do something like that.

Women are often treated like objects, and many men do not understand how their actions can affect them. Heela could not have an abortion because it might kill her, and if she kept the baby, she could no longer continue school. She decided to keep the baby with support from people around her, but she had to stop her education to take care of her newborn.

Some men do not see the consequences of their actions and run away when something goes wrong. This can prevent women from following their dreams or passions. I think we live in a generation where some men do not respect women, and that is unjust. We are all equal, and consent matters. Men need to treat women with respect and understand how their actions can affect others.
